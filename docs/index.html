Built for actually falling asleep

Not a meditation course. Not a subscription funnel. A sound mixer and a timer.

🎚️

Layer three at once

Pin up to three stations and balance them independently until the mix is yours.

🔊

Six sounds never loop

Noise and textures are synthesized live on your device rather than played from a file, so they never develop the audible loop point that gives most noise apps away at 3am.

⏱️

A timer that fades

5 to 60 minutes, plus a "15 more minutes" button. The mix fades out instead of stopping dead, so the timer never wakes you on its way out.

🌙

Night mode

Dims the whole interface, so checking the timer in a dark bedroom is not like taking a flashlight to the face.

🔒

Nothing leaves

No account, no analytics, no server. The app cannot collect data because it has nowhere to send it.

✈️

Works offline

Every sound is generated or bundled on device. No streaming, no buffering, no connection needed.

🔒 There is nothing to collect

Privacy here is not a policy decision. It is an architectural one.

✓No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to.
✓No analytics or ad SDKs. None are linked into the app.
✓No server. There is no backend to send anything to.
✓No microphone. The app never asks for it and never listens.
✓No health or sleep data. HealthKit is never accessed.
✓Your settings stay on device and are deleted with the app.
