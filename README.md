**Blend ambient sounds into your own mix and drift off on a timer. No account, no tracking, nothing leaves your device.**

[![Download on the App Store](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download_on_the-App_Store-0d96f6?style=for-the-badge&logo=apple&logoColor=white)](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6790789119)

**Buy once, keep forever.** No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases.

Most sleep apps hand you a fixed playlist and a monthly bill. Dreamwave Radio hands you a mixing desk. Layer rain over a fireplace, put a fan under a night train, and set the level of each one independently until it sounds like the room you actually want to fall asleep in.

## ✨ What Makes It Different

### 🎚️ Mix up to three sounds at once

Not a playlist - a mix. Pin up to three stations and set each level independently. Rain on a tent over distant thunder. Night wind under a fireplace. The combination is the point.

### 💾 Mixes, one tap away

Start with five ready-made mixes: Rainy Night, Focus, Gentle Sleep, Neon Coast and Midnight Rain. Or keep your own: pin up to three sounds, set each level, and tap the bookmark next to Play. Every mix has its own cover, and you can give any saved mix a photo of your own. Your mixes open as a grid of covers, and one tap brings any of them back, levels included.

### 📚 For focus and study

The same sounds work by day. Brown noise under rain on a window covers chatter in an open office, a café or a shared apartment, so you can focus, study or read.

### 🔊 Quiet all night

No clicks, no audible seam. Your sounds keep playing evenly for as long as you need them, so a long night stays one calm, continuous sound instead of a track that keeps starting over. Night Wind rises and falls in gusts. Distant Rain lets every drop land somewhere new. Cat Purr pauses when a cat would.

### 🗣️ Just ask

"Play Rain on Tent in Dreamwave." "Give me more time in Dreamwave." Siri and Shortcuts start your sound and stretch the timer without you having to look at a bright screen. Siri understands you in all nine languages of the app, and everything Siri can do is also a Shortcut, so a sound can start as part of your bedtime routine.

### ⏱️ A sleep timer that fades

Presets for the usual nights, or drag the scale to any duration from 1 to 120 minutes. The mix fades out rather than stopping dead, so the timer never wakes you up on its way out. While it runs it sits on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so a glance tells you how long is left.

### 🌙 Night mode

Dims the whole interface for a dark bedroom, so checking the timer does not mean taking a flashlight to the face.

### 📱 At home on iPhone and iPad

One app, built for both. On iPhone it stays reachable one-handed in the dark. On iPad the sound library opens full screen, so the whole library is in front of you at once.

### 🎧 Plays along with podcasts

Turn on "Play Along With Other Audio" in the settings, and your mix plays quietly under a podcast or an audiobook instead of stopping it. Off by default.

### 🔒 No account, no network, no data

There is no sign-up, no analytics, and no server, and the app never asks for your microphone. A photo you pick as a mix cover stays on your device. The app does not collect data because it has nowhere to send it. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy_policy.md).

## ❓ FAQ

**Why is the app called Dreamwave on my home screen?**

Since 2.0 the label under the icon is simply Dreamwave. The app itself is still Dreamwave Radio, in the App Store and inside the app, and Siri understands both names.

**Do my saved mixes stay after the update?**

Yes. Your saved mixes stay exactly as they are. The two new ready-made mixes, Neon Coast and Midnight Rain, are added as long as there is room, and you can give each mix a photo of your own as its cover.

**Does 2.0 cost anything if I already own the app?**

No. 2.0 is a free update for everyone who already has Dreamwave Radio. Buy once, keep forever: all sounds and all future sounds are included.

**Does the app need access to my photos?**

No. When you choose a photo as a mix cover, the system photo picker hands the app only that one photo, never your library. It stays on your device and is never uploaded.

**Does it work offline?**

Yes, completely. Every sound plays right on your device, so it works in airplane mode, on a train or in a hotel without Wi-Fi.

**Can it help with tinnitus?**

Many people use soft noise to make ringing in the ears less noticeable, and Dreamwave Radio has seven noise colors, so you can find the one that feels right to you. It is not a medical device and does not treat tinnitus. If your ears ring often or the ringing is new, please see a doctor.
