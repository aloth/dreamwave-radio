# Privacy Policy for Dreamwave Radio

**Effective Date:** August 3, 2026

Dreamwave Radio ("the App"), operated by Alexander Loth ("we," "us," or "our"), is designed so that there is nothing to collect. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information when you use the App.

We comply with applicable privacy laws and regulations including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

## 1. Our Guiding Principle: We Collect Nothing

Dreamwave Radio does not collect, store, track, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK. The App has no server component of any kind.

## 2. Information We Handle

### 2.1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores your preferences on your device only:

- Which sounds you have pinned to your mix
- The volume level set for each sound
- Sleep timer and night mode settings

This information never leaves your device. We have no access to it.

### 2.2. Audio Playback

All audio is produced on your device. Some sounds are synthesized in real time by the App; the others are audio files bundled inside the App itself. No audio is streamed, downloaded, or requested from any server.

### 2.3. Microphone

The App does not request or use microphone access. It does not listen to you.

### 2.4. Health and Sleep Data

The App does not request or access HealthKit, sleep tracking, or any other health data.

## 3. Network Access

The App does not require an internet connection to function and does not make network requests during normal use.

The only exception is when you deliberately tap an external link in the About screen, such as the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, or the support email address. Those links open in Safari or Mail, at which point Apple's apps and the destination website apply their own terms. We receive nothing from this.

## 4. Sharing of Your Information

We do not share any data, because we do not have any.

- **No Sale of Personal Data:** We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We never receive it.
- **No Third-Party Sharing:** Your settings stay on your device.
- **No Analytics or Advertising:** The App contains no analytics, attribution, tracking, or advertising frameworks.
- **Legal Requirements:** Were we compelled by law to disclose user data, we would have none to disclose.

## 5. Purchases

Any purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 6. Data Security

All data stays in the App's sandboxed container on your device, protected by the security model built into iOS. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no data in transit to intercept and no database of ours to breach.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. Since the App collects no data at all, no data from children is collected either.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Your privacy rights are fully respected because your data never leaves your possession.

- **Access, Correction, Deletion:** All settings are visible and changeable inside the App. Deleting the App removes all associated data from your device permanently.
- **Portability:** There is no account or server-side profile to export.

## 9. Data Retention

Settings remain on your device until you change them or uninstall the App. We retain nothing, because we receive nothing.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted at this address and the "Effective Date" above will be revised. We encourage you to review it periodically.
